Megas Moves Android App Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 3 November 2026 · App: Megas Moves: AI Coding Agents · Package: com.amretra.megasmoves

Amretra Dynamics operates the Megas Moves Android application and its related services. This notice explains how we access, collect, use, share, secure, retain, and delete user and device data. It covers the Android app, including its connections to an authorized Megas Moves Worker, rather than only the public marketing website. Some features described here are optional or available only in certain app versions.

Privacy controls and account deletion

Use app settings and Android system settings to manage optional permissions and device connections. You can request deletion of your account and eligible associated data without the app through our account deletion page.

1. Information we process

  • Account and authentication: email address, display name if provided, Firebase Authentication identifiers, authentication state, and account/security events.
  • Devices and connectivity: device and Worker identifiers, device or operating-system version, app version, IP-derived connection information, supported capabilities, pairing state/history, authorization grants, and connection events.
  • Workflow content you choose or authorize: prompts, messages, agent responses, selected files or attachments, commands, task metadata, results, terminal or browser excerpts, observations, approvals, and diagnostic evidence as necessary for the selected feature. This may include personal data contained in the content you choose to process.
  • Operations and diagnostics: timestamps, crash/error information where available, app or task status, notifications, delivery records, and security or permission audit events.
  • Subscriptions if used: plan, product and purchase identifiers, purchase token or receipt reference, payment state, and entitlement information. Full payment-card credentials are processed by the payment provider rather than stored by Megas Moves.
  • Support: messages and information you submit when requesting help, reporting a problem, or seeking account deletion.

2. Optional Android permissions and sensitive access

Accessibility service. If you explicitly enable the optional Megas Moves phone-control accessibility service in Android system settings, the app can read visible UI content and interface descriptions in a user-selected app and perform bounded user-approved interface actions. Visible content can contain sensitive personal information. The implementation restricts password and protected system/security surfaces and uses account, device, and session approval checks. Enabling the accessibility service alone does not grant an unrelated remote party control. The feature is for authorized owner-directed operations, not an advertised disability-support accessibility tool. You can disable the service in Android accessibility settings and revoke an active control session.

Microphone and audio. If you give microphone permission and use an audio-enabled feature, audio input may be processed to fulfill your request, including transmission to the explicitly selected service/provider when needed. You can deny or revoke microphone access in Android settings.

Notifications and connected-device services. With your permission, the app may show work results, connection or security notifications and maintain expressly enabled background connections. Notification details depend on settings and Android permissions.

Files, browsing and connected computers. Only files or content you select, share, or authorize for a workflow may be processed by that workflow. Third-party applications, browsers, repositories, websites, devices, and AI providers have their own permissions and privacy terms.

3. Purposes of processing

We use the above information to authenticate accounts, verify ownership, pair and connect devices, carry out approved tasks, route and synchronize selected workflow context, support notifications and purchases, enforce permission and security controls, prevent fraud or abuse, troubleshoot problems, respond to support requests, and comply with legal requirements.

4. Local processing, remote transfer and sharing

A Megas Moves Worker can run on a computer you own or are authorized to manage. Local files, terminal state, browser profiles, repositories and other Worker data may remain on that computer. When you explicitly enable a connected feature, selected content or connection metadata may be transmitted over authenticated connections between the Android app, the paired Worker, the service backend and any third-party service you select. A connected operation does not mean every local file is automatically uploaded.

Categories of service providers may include Google Firebase for authentication and messaging; Google Play for integrity and billing where used; infrastructure, hosting, notification, diagnostics or customer-support providers needed to operate the product; and coding agent, model, repository, browser or remote-computer services you elect to connect. These providers process the information required for the particular operation and may have independent privacy notices. We do not sell personal data for money or use payment data for behavioral advertising.

We may disclose data when required by valid legal process, to protect users or systems, or when an authorized service transition requires it with applicable safeguards. Data may be processed outside your country where the chosen service operates; applicable legal safeguards apply.

5. Storage, retention and security

We use measures appropriate to the service, including authenticated connections, owner-scoped permissions, transport encryption, restrictions on secrets, and security logging. No system is guaranteed secure. Protect your devices, credentials, connected services and recovery methods.

Retention varies by the type of record and purpose: active account and connected-service operation; support, security and audit integrity; dispute resolution; and applicable accounting or legal duties. Following a verified deletion request, eligible account and product data is deleted or de-identified from active systems. Some records may remain when legally required or necessary for fraud prevention, security, tax, disputes or valid legal claims. Backup copies follow their applicable retention processes, and connected third parties may have separate retention practices.

6. Your choices, rights and deletion

You can deny or revoke optional Android permissions, stop a control session, disconnect or revoke paired devices and Workers, and manage connected services. Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, export or deletion of your personal information, withdraw consent to optional processing, or raise a privacy grievance.

Account deletion: Use the deletion option in the Android app's Profile or Account Settings where supported, or request deletion without the app using our public account deletion instructions. We verify ownership before completing deletion. Uninstalling the app does not automatically remove associated account data. Deleting an account does not automatically cancel a subscription managed independently by Google Play or another payment provider; cancel that subscription with its provider.

7. Children and policy updates

Megas Moves is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly solicit data from children. We may update this policy when the app, its data practices or legal requirements change, and will provide additional notice when required.
