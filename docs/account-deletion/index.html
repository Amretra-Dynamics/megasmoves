Delete your Megas Moves account

Updated 3 November 2026 · Applies to Megas Moves: AI Coding Agents (com.amretra.megasmoves)

You can request deletion of your account and eligible associated personal data even if the Android app is no longer installed or you cannot sign in.

Request deletion without the app

Email master@megasmoves.io with the subject Megas Moves Android account deletion request. Include the email address associated with your account and state that you want your Megas Moves account and associated data deleted. Do not include passwords, tokens, one-time codes, private keys or pairing secrets.

We may ask you to verify account ownership before taking irreversible action. You do not need to reinstall the app to make the request.

Deletion from within the Android app

Where supported by your installed version, sign in and open Profile or Account Settings, select the permanent account-deletion option and follow the on-screen confirmation. If you cannot access the app, email us using the steps above.

Which data is deleted

After verification, eligible Megas Moves account identifiers and profile data, owned-device and pairing associations, saved workflow data, and related personal product records are removed or de-identified from active systems as appropriate. Data on a separate computer, local Worker or third-party service may also require deletion on that device or with that provider.

Which data may be retained

Limited information may need to be retained for legal or tax obligations, security and fraud prevention, payment reconciliation, valid disputes, backup retention or protection of others' rights. We will explain material exceptions as appropriate. We do not retain eligible account data merely to keep a deleted profile active.

Subscriptions, permissions and connected devices

Revoking your owned-device and Worker connections is recommended when closing your account. Account deletion does not automatically cancel independently billed Google Play or third-party subscriptions. Cancel any such subscription with its billing provider to stop future renewals.

Questions or grievances

Contact master@megasmoves.io for help with requests, access, export or correction. Developer: Amretra Dynamics, India.
