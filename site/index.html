01 / THE APPROACH

Power should come
with boundaries.

Useful software doesn't need unlimited permission. MegasMoves is being built around scoped access, observable actions, and the user staying in control.

01
⌁

Local by design

Work starts near your own tools and projects. The Community preview inspects only folders you explicitly add.
